# Job Prediction Service - phase1
Job Prediction Service Phase 1- In the first phase,we are Researching, Collecting raw data, cleaning the dataset and Exploratory data analysis(EDA).

**Data Source:-** The Data is taken from Kaggle site for offering analysis on the “LinkedIn job posting-2023” data. This dataset contains a comprehensive record of 15,000+ job postings listed over the course of 2 days. Each posting contains 27 features or attributes like, title, job description, salary, work-types etc.

**Background:-** 
LinkedIn is one of the largest platforms for job postings, widely utilized by companies to attract talent and by applicants to explore career opportunities. However, navigating the job market presents challenges for various stakeholders.
 • **Job Seekers & Students:** The rapidly evolving job market makes it difficult for job seekers, especially students, to find suitable roles or internships. Understanding the demand for 
 specific skill sets, industry trends, and salary benchmarks is crucial for making informed career decisions.
 • **Companies & HR Professionals:** Organizations risk losing top talent if their salary structures and benefits are not competitive. HR professionals must strategically plan recruitment 
 efforts and optimize job postings based on current labor market conditions.
 • **Government & Economic Planners:** Policymakers rely on workforce trends to shape economic development strategies and employment policies effectively.
 • **Impact of COVID-19:** The job market has undergone significant transformation due to the COVID-19 pandemic, leading to a surge in remote work opportunities and shifting hiring        
 practices.

This project aims to address these challenges by providing data-driven insights into job market trends, helping job seekers, companies, and policymakers make informed decisions.

**Outcome:-**
It could give insight on the hiring process post pandemic like job roles, skillset in demand, remote jobs, sponsorship offered to help both seekers as well as employers.
• The government can also make informed decisions from the current job trends or employment offered in their region by implementing policies for companies and others to address unemployment and other market challenges.
• The educators and training programs can take help of the analysis to offer the skills that are in demand and make the job seekers more prepared.it also helps the employers to find suitable candidates for the job offered.
• HR Professional can also make use of the analysis to tailor their job offerings against other industry and can also find where the job seekers are more interested.
This provides crucial information that helps the labor market to make efficient decisions in the rapidly evolving labor market.
